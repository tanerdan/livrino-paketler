Help & FAQ

Reading

What do Full, B1 and A2 mean?

Full is the complete text: the author's own sentences or our full translation. B1 and A2 tell the same scenes in simpler language for learners; A2 is the easiest. You can switch language and level at any time with the selectors at the top.

Which languages can I read in?

Books can be read in Turkish, English, German and Spanish. The book page shows which levels each language has.

What is dual text (+TR)?

It shows the sentence in a language you know in small type under the balloon. If you get stuck while reading in the language you are learning, you see the meaning right away.

How do I listen to a balloon?

Tap the ▶ button next to the balloon; tap again to stop. Tap the balloon itself to open it in every language, with audio and the glossary. Audio is a Plus feature.

Where is the glossary?

When you read a book in a language other than its original one, the harder words of each panel appear as chips under it. Tap a chip to see the meaning and hear the word. The glossary is a Plus feature.

What are the notes marked ℹ?

Culture notes: short explanations of historical or cultural references in the text (for example "Damon and Pythias" or "Scotland Yard"). They are free and available in every language.

How do I change the app language?

Use the language buttons at the top of the bookshelf. The language of buttons and menus is independent of the language you read in.

Chapters

Which chapters are free?

The first 2 chapters of every book are open in all languages and levels, with audio.

Can I read later chapters for free?

Yes. In every book you can unlock the next chapter for free once every 24 hours. These chapters open in order and are read in the Full text; B1/A2, audio and the glossary come with Plus. If you like, we notify you when your next free chapter is ready.

Why are books with a "New" and "Plus" badge locked?

New books are open only to Plus members for their first 7 days. After that, the rules above apply to everyone.

What happens when I tap a chapter marked 🔒?

A screen with the Plus and book purchase options opens. You can close it without committing to anything.

Plus and purchases

What does Livrino Plus unlock?

Every chapter of every book, the B1 and A2 levels, audio, the glossary, and access to new books in their first 7 days.

Which options are there?
  • Monthly Plus
  • Yearly Plus: starts with a 14-day free trial
  • Single book: all chapters and features of that book, forever

Prices appear in your own currency on the purchase screen. Payment is handled by Google Play.

What happens when the trial ends?

The yearly plan starts automatically and is charged to your Google Play account. We remind you 2 days before the trial ends and 2 days before each yearly renewal, so you can cancel before then if you wish.

How do I cancel my subscription?
  1. Open the Google Play Store and tap your profile picture at the top right.
  2. Go to Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.
  3. Choose Livrino › Cancel subscription.

After cancelling, Plus stays active until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

I changed phones or reinstalled the app. Where are my purchases?

Open the app with the same Google account and your purchases come back by themselves. If they don't, tap "Restore purchases" on the purchase screen.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are requested from Google under Google Play's policies. If you have a problem, write to destek@livrino.app and we'll help.

Downloads and storage

Can I read without internet?

Yes. Downloaded chapters stay on your phone and open offline. The first chapter of the first book comes with the app.

How do I download chapters and audio?

Tap Download on the book page. Audio comes in separate packs per language; in the "⋯ Downloads & audio" menu you can download only the language you want.

I want to free up space.

In the same menu you can delete a language's audio or the whole book. You can download it again later; your progress and purchases are not lost.

Are books updated?

Yes. When we fix a chapter (image, translation, audio), the app downloads the new version by itself when it opens. You can also refresh by pulling the bookshelf down.

Reporting problems, contact and privacy

I found a mistake in a panel.

Tap the ⚑ button under the panel, choose what is wrong and add a short note if you like. Reports come straight to us and fixes reach the app in later updates. Without internet, the report is sent once you are back online.

How can I contact you?

Write to destek@livrino.app. We usually answer within 2 working days.

What data do you collect?

You don't need an account. An anonymous device ID is used for purchase records. Problem reports send the panel, language, level and screen width; your name and e-mail are not sent. Details and how to have your data deleted: livrino.app/gizlilik.
