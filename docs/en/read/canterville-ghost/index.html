What the book is about

American diplomat Mr Otis and his family buy an English manor famous for its centuries-old ghost. Sir Simon puts on his most terrifying performances, but this practical family answers with stain remover and oil for his chains, and the twins play tricks on him. Oscar Wilde's funny and finally touching story about the old world meeting the new.
