What the book is about

Nobody at the Ankara office really knows Raif Efendi, a quiet, unremarkable clerk. When the young narrator reads a notebook left in his drawer, a different man appears: a lonely young Turk in 1920s Berlin, a portrait of a woman in a fur coat seen at an exhibition, and Maria Puder, the painter behind it. One of the most-read love stories in Turkish literature, about loneliness, courage and missed moments.

About the author: Sabahattin Ali

Sabahattin Ali (1907–1948) was a Turkish short-story writer, novelist and poet. A teacher and translator, he wrote plainly about lonely people and quiet sorrows.
