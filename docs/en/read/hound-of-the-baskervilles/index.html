What the book is about

Sir Charles Baskerville is found dead in the grounds of his Dartmoor manor, with the footprints of a gigantic hound nearby. According to the family legend, a hound from hell haunts the Baskervilles. Sherlock Holmes and Dr Watson go to the misty moors to protect the heir, Sir Henry, and find the truth behind the legend. Conan Doyle's most famous Sherlock Holmes novel.
