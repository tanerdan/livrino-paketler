What the book is about

In London, the respectable lawyer Utterson finds a strange name in his friend Dr Jekyll's will: Edward Hyde, a man who tramples a child in the street and fills everyone with disgust. What could link a respected doctor to such a man? A step-by-step investigation through foggy streets, and one of the most famous stories ever told about the good and evil sides of a person.

About the author: Robert Louis Stevenson

Robert Louis Stevenson (1850–1894) was a Scottish writer, also the author of Treasure Island. Often ill, he travelled the world and wrote about adventure and the dark side of people.
