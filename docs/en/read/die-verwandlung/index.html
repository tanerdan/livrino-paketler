What the book is about

Travelling salesman Gregor Samsa wakes one morning to find himself turned into a huge insect. His first worry is not his body but missing the train and losing his job, because his family depends on him. How will his mother, father and sister behind the door cope with this new life? Kafka's short, unforgettable story about family, duty and alienation.

About the author: Franz Kafka

Franz Kafka (1883–1924) was an insurance clerk in Prague who wrote in German. Most of his work appeared after his death; he described loneliness and absurd systems in a voice like no other.
