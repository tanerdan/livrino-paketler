What the book is about

Summer in St Petersburg, the season of white nights when it never gets dark. A lonely young man who has wandered the city for years without speaking to anyone sees Nastenka crying by a canal one night and goes to her. They promise to meet at the same spot the next night. Over four nights they share their secrets: his daydreams, and the person Nastenka is waiting for. One of Dostoevsky's shortest and most moving stories, about loneliness and hope.
