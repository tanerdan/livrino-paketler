How it works

Livrino turns classic works of literature into vertical comics. Read the same scene in English, Turkish, German or Spanish; switch the text between full, B1 and A2, listen to every balloon and tap hard words. A real story instead of textbook sentences.

  1. Pick two languages

    Choose the language you want to read and a second language for help; it appears in small type under each balloon.

  2. Pick your level

    Full text is the author's own words. B1 and A2 retell the same scenes in simpler language; switch at any time.

  3. Listen and tap

    Hear every balloon read aloud and tap difficult words for their meaning. Culture notes explain the references.

Frequently asked questions

What do Full, B1 and A2 mean?

Full is the complete text: the author's own sentences or our full translation. B1 and A2 tell the same scenes in simpler language for learners; A2 is the easiest. You can switch language and level at any time with the selectors at the top.

Which languages can I read in?

Books can be read in Turkish, English, German and Spanish. The book page shows which levels each language has.

Which chapters are free?

The first 2 chapters of every book are open in all languages and levels, with audio.
